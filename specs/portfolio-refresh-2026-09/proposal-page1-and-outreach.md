## 5. Adversarial pass on the above

1. **Renaming can erase credibility.** Doczy is the one name with third-party proof (AWS blog, patent). Renaming it would orphan those references. Keep it; rename only names with no public footprint.
2. **Renaming breaks internal keys and links.** FEATURED matches on name substrings; deep links are name slugs. Any rename must update FEATURED and accept that yesterday's `#p=` links die. Cheap, but must be done together.
3. **Percent KPIs will not impress an investor.** Six of the proposed ten lead with a percentage. Where a dollar or scale figure exists, put it first (see tweak above). Where it does not, say so honestly rather than inventing one.
4. **Brane AI at 02 invites "how many customers?"** The honest answer is none yet; the entry already avoids production claims. In the DM say "just built", not "shipped product". If that feels weak, swap 02 and 03 so the 100× engine sits second and Brane AI third; the founder lane still shows above the fold.
5. **Lane (b) is the weaker fit.** She wants operators who have scaled companies; 25 people and $625K in grants is real but small. Lead with lane (a), offer lane (b) as "what I'd want to run". Do not describe yourself as a serial CEO.
6. **The 100× number will be probed by an agentic engineer.** Basis to have ready: 394 cases from 102 rules regenerate in minutes from one command versus weeks of hand-authoring for the previous package. If you cannot defend the manual baseline in a call, soften to "orders of magnitude faster".
7. **GitHub is the weak link in the Engineer lens.** The profile README still says 30+ systems, 110+ agents, 443 citations, h-index 9. The account has 21 public forks, including tutorial repos and a "linkedin-skill-assessments-quizzes" fork, which read as clutter to a technical GP. Fix before sending: update the README numbers (150+ / 460 / 10), archive or delete the forks, keep the six pinned repos (already good ones).
8. **She will get hundreds of DMs.** Differentiators that survive a skim: one verifiable dollar figure in the first line, one link that loads fast, and a concrete offer (30-minute live walkthrough). The draft keeps all three in the first screen.
9. **Recency matters.** The post was 46 minutes old when photographed. Comment today; DM today. A polished site next week is worth less than a good DM now, so send the DM before the renames land.
10. **Spelling.** Falon, not Felon or Felpn. Digital Indies, TIGER 21.
11. **Do not tailor the site to her by name or phrase.** Everything in §3 is generic to any operator-investor reader. That is what you asked for and it also reads better to everyone else.
12. **Confidentiality.** The DM cites only figures already public on the site and resume. No client names, no repo access. Nothing new leaves the machine.
